The exact workflow this is for

Not generic expense management. The receipt already exists in Xero, the expense is rebilled to a client, and the evidence still has to be reconstructed manually.

Current loop
  1. Open each source bill or expense.
  2. Find and download the receipt.
  3. Repeat for every rebilled line.
  4. Count and reconcile files.
  5. Upload them again to the customer invoice.
What we are testing
  1. Select a Xero sales invoice.
  2. Resolve linked billable expenses.
  3. Find existing evidence.
  4. Flag anything missing.
  5. Create one indexed evidence pack.
  6. Attach it back to Xero.
The wedge is intentionally narrow: do not replace Xero, Dext, Hubdoc or receipt capture. Remove the duplicate work after the evidence is already in Xero.

Xero Rebillable Expense Evidence Checklist

CheckWhat to verify
1. Rebillable linesMatch every client-recharged cost to its source bill, spend-money transaction or expense.
2. Evidence existsConfirm each recharged cost has the relevant supplier invoice, receipt, ticket, docket or other required proof.
3. Amount & currencyVerify evidence supports the amount being recharged and any FX treatment or markup is clear.
4. Client/projectConfirm every source cost belongs to the correct client, project or job.
5. Remove duplicatesExclude duplicate files, internal-only pages and irrelevant material.
6. Evidence orderArrange documents in the same sequence as rebilled lines where possible.
7. ReconcileCount rebilled expense lines vs supported lines and review any mismatch before sending.
